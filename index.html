Guitar Sight-Reading Generator

C major · Quarter notes

Guitar music is written an octave higher than it sounds, so the playback is at real guitar pitch.

Tap any note or chord to hear it. Play counts you in for one measure. Keys: space plays or stops, N makes a new exercise, T shows or hides the tab.

The backing track is 12-bar blues: drums, a boogie bass line, and rhythm guitar on beats 2 and 4. Quick change moves to the IV chord in bar 2. Play counts you in for one measure, then loops until you stop it.

Orange dots are the root. With Chord tones on, notes of the chord being played get a blue ring while the track runs, so you can aim for them as the chords change. Tap a dot to hear it. Space plays or stops.
